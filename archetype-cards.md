# Archetype Cards

These are the source-of-truth profiles for the Custom GPT.

## Guide

**Tagline:** Keeps the team safe enough to grow and honest enough to heal.  
**Definition:** A people-first stabilizer who builds trust, clarity, and cohesion when teams need steadiness more than force.

```text
      /^\
  ___/   \___
 (  o   o   )
  \   ^    /
   |  ~   |__
  /|_____ /  \
 /_/  |  / /\ \
    _/  /_/  \_\
```

**Signature abilities**
- `Trust Lantern`
- `Conflict Softener`
- `Steady Presence`

**Core leverage**
- Creates psychological safety without losing the thread of accountability.
- Helps teams recover from trust debt, attrition, or leadership inconsistency.

**Best-fit contexts**
- Morale repair
- Team reset after conflict
- New manager transitions
- Fragile or high-anxiety teams

**Works well with**
- `Driver`: adds safety and retention around urgency
- `Operator`: pairs empathy with structure
- `Mentor`: compounds growth and development

**Friction risks**
- May frustrate `Driver` when speed is paramount
- May be misread by `Builder` as too indirect
- Can overprotect low performers in chaotic teams

**Needs around them**
- A `Driver` or `Operator` when execution urgency is real

**Shadow form:** `The People Pleaser`
- Triggered by conflict, fear of fallout, or fragile team morale
- Avoids hard calls, softens feedback too much, protects comfort over standards
- Team impact: drift, unclear accountability, low performer shielding
- Recovery: state the hard truth plainly, name one standard being protected, and follow through on one uncomfortable decision

## Operator

**Tagline:** Turns disorder into cadence, coordination, and dependable delivery.  
**Definition:** A results-minded stabilizer who builds the rhythms, rules, and interfaces that keep teams shipping predictably.

```text
      .----.
  ___/ []  \___
 /   _  __     \
 |  | ||  | [] |
 |  | ||  |    |
 |__|_||__|____|
   /_/  \_\
  /_/    \_\
```

**Signature abilities**
- `Cadence Forge`
- `Dependency Radar`
- `Execution Grid`

**Core leverage**
- Reduces entropy through operating systems rather than heroics.
- Makes multi-team execution less fragile.

**Best-fit contexts**
- Scaling teams
- Delivery inconsistency
- Coordination-heavy environments
- Compliance or reliability pressure

**Works well with**
- `Driver`: turns momentum into repeatable execution
- `Builder`: helps technical force become scalable
- `Steward`: strengthens continuity and follow-through

**Friction risks**
- May frustrate `Catalyst` by slowing change
- May be misread by `Guide` as too process-heavy
- Can become defensive when asked to break working systems quickly

**Needs around them**
- A `Catalyst` or `Driver` when the org needs change, not just order

**Shadow form:** `The Bureaucrat`
- Triggered by risk, scale pain, or fear of failure
- Adds process reflexively, confuses control with progress
- Team impact: slower decisions, paperwork over outcomes, local optimization
- Recovery: remove one nonessential control, reconnect process to an actual failure mode, and simplify ownership

## Driver

**Tagline:** Creates movement, pressure, and visible progress when speed matters.  
**Definition:** A change-and-results leader who clears paths, sets pace, and pushes teams toward outcomes with urgency.

```text
      /\ 
  __ /  \__
 / _\_||_/_ \
 |/  o  o \|
  \   --  /
   \_>< _/__
    /||\   /
   /_||_\_/
```

**Signature abilities**
- `Momentum Call`
- `Priority Blade`
- `Finish Line Pull`

**Core leverage**
- Converts ambiguity into focused movement.
- Helps teams break stalls and recover delivery confidence fast.

**Best-fit contexts**
- Turnarounds
- Deadlined launches
- Performance recovery
- Feature-velocity pushes

**Works well with**
- `Operator`: grounds speed in systems
- `Guide`: protects people while pace rises
- `Builder`: accelerates execution in hard technical terrain

**Friction risks**
- May steamroll `Guide`
- May outpace `Operator`
- Can burn out teams that actually need healing or clarity

**Needs around them**
- A `Guide` or `Steward` to prevent exhaustion and trust debt

**Shadow form:** `The Tyrant`
- Triggered by missed goals, external pressure, or prolonged ambiguity
- Becomes controlling, impatient, and output-obsessed
- Team impact: burnout, silence, fear-driven execution, shallow decisions
- Recovery: shorten the priority list, re-open dissent, and reset pace around sustainable throughput

## Catalyst

**Tagline:** Reorients the team with vision, energy, and a story people can move behind.  
**Definition:** A people-and-change leader who creates strategic motion through narrative, alignment, and possibility.

```text
        .-.
    _  (   )  _
   / \ /_._\ / \
  /_  /  |  \  _\
    \/ __|__ \/
      /  |  \
   __/___|___\__
  /_____/ \_____\
```

**Signature abilities**
- `Vision Spark`
- `Narrative Lift`
- `Alignment Shift`

**Core leverage**
- Helps people believe in and move toward a new direction.
- Useful when the real bottleneck is not effort but alignment.

**Best-fit contexts**
- Reorgs
- Pivots
- Innovation resets
- Culture repair through shared purpose

**Works well with**
- `Strategist`: turns concept into longer-range shape
- `Mentor`: helps people adapt to change
- `Operator`: closes the loop on change execution

**Friction risks**
- May frustrate `Operator` if change outruns closure
- May be misread by `Builder` as all talk
- Can create motion without sufficient finish discipline

**Needs around them**
- An `Operator` or `Steward` to anchor movement in follow-through

**Shadow form:** `The Vision Storm`
- Triggered by stagnation, politics, or impatience with inertia
- Launches ideas faster than systems can absorb them
- Team impact: churn, fatigue, broken trust in leadership narratives
- Recovery: narrow to one change story, define what stops as well as what starts, and bind change to visible follow-through

## Builder

**Tagline:** Turns ambiguity into systems, decisions, and shipped work.  
**Definition:** A technical player-coach who leads through direct engineering leverage while still carrying leadership responsibility.

```text
       ___
   ___/o o\___
  /__   ^   __\
    /|===|\ 
 __/_|===|_\__
/___/|___|\___\
    /_/ \_\
   /_/   \_\
```

**Signature abilities**
- `Direct Unblock`
- `Architectural Gravity`
- `Crisis Hands`

**Core leverage**
- Collapses technical ambiguity quickly.
- Raises confidence in hard systems work by staying close to the actual engineering.

**Best-fit contexts**
- Startups
- Technical turnarounds
- Thin teams with hard architecture work
- Incident-heavy environments

**Works well with**
- `Operator`: scales direct force into a system
- `Driver`: pairs urgency with technical traction
- `Strategist`: connects implementation to larger direction

**Friction risks**
- May intimidate `Guide`
- May create hidden dependency in the team
- Can over-own hard problems rather than building others up

**Needs around them**
- A `Guide` or `Mentor` to keep leadership from collapsing into rescue work

**Shadow form:** `The Hero`
- Triggered by weak ownership, technical danger, or fear of failure
- Jumps in personally, solves too much directly, becomes indispensable
- Team impact: dependency, bottlenecks, unclear growth paths, manager overload
- Recovery: delegate one critical path, leave one problem unsolved by yourself, and turn direct intervention into teachable structure

## Steward

**Tagline:** Protects continuity, reliability, and low-drama progress over time.  
**Definition:** A stability-first leader who preserves team health, operational sanity, and durable systems when constant volatility is the real threat.

```text
       ___
   ___/   \___
  /  o\ /o   \
 |      ^     |
 |   .---.    |
 |__/|___|\___|
   /_/   \_\
  /_/_____\_\
```

**Signature abilities**
- `Reliability Ward`
- `Boundary Keeper`
- `Continuity Sense`

**Core leverage**
- Keeps the team from being ground down by chaos, interruptions, or chronic overreach.
- Defends the conditions needed for good work to continue.

**Best-fit contexts**
- Reliability-sensitive teams
- Long-lived platforms
- Teams recovering from repeated thrash
- High-obligation service ownership

**Works well with**
- `Operator`: strengthens durable execution
- `Guide`: protects people and standards together
- `Strategist`: stabilizes long-range change

**Friction risks**
- May frustrate `Driver` as too cautious
- May be misread by `Catalyst` as resistant
- Can preserve systems past their useful life

**Needs around them**
- A `Driver` or `Catalyst` when necessary change is overdue

**Shadow form:** `The Hoverer`
- Triggered by risk accumulation, production fear, or team mistrust
- Oversees everything too closely and constrains autonomy
- Team impact: slower ownership growth, decision drag, low initiative
- Recovery: define explicit guardrails, step back from one control point, and allow a bounded miss without reclaiming everything

**Tagline:** Grows capability faster than most people think is possible.  
**Definition:** A people-development leader who multiplies talent, confidence, and leadership capacity through deliberate coaching.

```text
  /  o    o  \
 |     /\     |
 |  .-====-.  |
 |_/  ||||  \_|
   /__||||__\
     /_  _\
```

**Signature abilities**
- `Growth Mirror`
- `Stretch Path`
- `Quiet Upgrade`

**Core leverage**
- Raises the ceiling of the team by accelerating individual growth.
- Especially useful when the org needs new leaders, not just more output.

**Best-fit contexts**
- Team formation
- Succession building
- High-potential teams
- New manager development

**Works well with**
- `Guide`: compounds trust and growth
- `Catalyst`: helps people absorb change
- `Strategist`: develops leaders for broader system needs

**Friction risks**
- May frustrate `Driver` by moving too indirectly
- May be misread by `Builder` as insufficiently concrete
- Can coach past the point where a hard call is needed

**Needs around them**
- A `Driver` or `Operator` when the system needs sharper execution pressure

**Shadow form:** `The Rescuer`
- Triggered by seeing potential, fear of discouraging people, or guilt around performance gaps
- Over-invests in helping, carries too much emotional or developmental load
- Team impact: blurred accountability, leader exhaustion, uneven standards
- Recovery: separate support from ownership, name one performance line clearly, and stop doing growth work for people who are not doing it themselves

## Strategist

**Tagline:** Sees the board, not just the next move.  
**Definition:** A system-level leader who creates leverage through direction, org design, and long-horizon technical judgment.

```text
        /^\
   ____/   \____
  /  o   o     /|
 /      ^     / |
 |  \_____/  |  |
 |___\===/___|__|
    /_/ \_\
   /_/   \_\
```

**Signature abilities**
- `System Map`
- `Leverage Sense`
- `Future Constraint Read`

**Core leverage**
- Spots structural issues before they become tactical emergencies.
- Helps teams make better tradeoffs across boundaries and time horizons.

**Best-fit contexts**
- Multi-team scale
- Platform or architecture evolution
- Org redesign
- Cross-functional leverage problems

**Works well with**
- `Catalyst`: combines direction with movement
- `Operator`: grounds strategy in operating reality
- `Builder`: links architecture to execution truth

**Friction risks**
- May frustrate `Driver` as too long-horizon
- May be misread by `Guide` as detached
- Can become abstract if too far from current execution

**Needs around them**
- A `Builder` or `Operator` to keep strategy tied to lived execution

**Shadow form:** `The Chessmaster`
- Triggered by organizational complexity, politics, or distance from day-to-day reality
- Over-indexes on positioning, frameworks, and indirect control
- Team impact: abstraction, low clarity, decisions that feel imposed from above
- Recovery: reconnect one strategic thesis to frontline pain, simplify the plan, and move one idea from concept into accountable execution
